Reading the chart. The dashed zero line is the median club each season; +700 means a team rated 700 Elo points above that middle. Values climb toward recent years partly because the whole field's ratings inflate over time — the median-relative scale here controls for that, so a rising line means pulling away from the pack, not just riding the tide.

Caveats. Rhino Slam! registered as "Rhino" in 2025; the two are merged here so the line stays continuous — a club-identity fix, distinct from player identity. Ratings are on the model's internal scale, not the USAU algorithm's. Built from 12,702 games and 97,423 roster entries scraped from play.usaultimate.org.
